# Part 2 – Computer Disassembly
## Before Opening the Computer
Describe the computer before opening it.
-a computer appears as a complete, enclosed device protected by an outer case. A desktop computer typically has a system unit with a power button, USB ports, and ventilation openings, while a laptop includes a screen, keyboard, touchpad, and built-in speakers. From the outside, only the user-accessible parts are visible, and the internal components remain hidden inside the casing.

## Internal Layout
Open the computer case and observe the internal components.
Describe what you see.
-After opening the computer, you can see its internal hardware components. These usually include the motherboard, processor (CPU) with a cooling fan or heat sink, RAM (memory) modules, storage drives such as an SSD or hard drive, the power supply unit, and various cables connecting the parts. Some computers also contain a graphics card, expansion cards, and additional cooling fans. All these components work together to process data and operate the computer efficiently.
